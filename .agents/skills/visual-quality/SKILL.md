# Visual Quality

Apply root `AGENTS.md` and the creator's own `BRAND.md` / approved visual brief. This Skill is a quality check, not a universal brand preset.

## Review order

1. User's explicit requirements and approved references.
2. Creator-specific brand tokens and prohibited treatments.
3. Source-footage constraints: face, hands, gestures, headroom, lighting and background.
4. Readability and hierarchy at the actual target-platform size.
5. Motion continuity, pacing and export integrity.

## Typography and captions

- Review persistent captions, kinetic typography and information overlays as separate layers with different purposes.
- Persistent captions must remain readable against the local background throughout the shot and across every reframe.
- Use the creator's specified caption colors and active-word treatment. Do not impose a universal highlight color.
- If the brief specifies monochrome captions, choose black or white according to local contrast. A restrained shadow or subtle glow may support separation only when allowed by the brief; do not add a stroke or opaque plate unless explicitly requested.
- Check word grouping, line length, safe zones, face clearance and collisions with headlines or graphic overlays.
- Kinetic headlines must use real approved wording. Production labels, placeholder copy and descriptions of an intended effect must never appear in the final video.

## Motion and composition

- Check that motion supports meaning and that transitions preserve spatial continuity.
- Review pacing for long unchanged visual states; use roughly 2–3 seconds as a review heuristic, not a mandatory animation quota.
- Check that repeated inserts or UI compositions are intentional rather than accidental duplication.
- Preserve natural subject scale, face, headroom, hands and meaningful gestures.
- Do not introduce colors, plates, strokes, shadows, gradients, frames or decorative elements that conflict with the brief.

## Audio and export

- Check audio continuity, synchronization, clipping indicators and abrupt tails where applicable.
- Distinguish automated audio metrics from actual human listening.
- Verify export dimensions, frame rate, codec, duration and full-file decode.
- Report what was inspected and what remains unverified. Do not claim a visual review or listening session that did not happen.
